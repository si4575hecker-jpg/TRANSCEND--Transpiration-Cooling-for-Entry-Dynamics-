In this prototype trajectory, the plan was to make starship slowly ignite the engines in small patterns slowly while re-entering to reduce velocity,
decreasing heat (skipping). This meathod also includes releasing small but effective amounts of coolant actively while descending to reduce heat 
corrosion on heat tiles (sweating). This served as the starting concept for this project. This could have been very effective, however this method 
was rejected as it exposed the hypothetical people inside the craft to extensive and possibly fatal amounts of g forces.

Hence this was rejected because of passenger safety concerns.
